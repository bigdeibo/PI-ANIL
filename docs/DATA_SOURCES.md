# Data sources and retrieval

This repository does **not** mirror third-party datasets. Each dataset is
obtained from its original source under its original terms, following the
practice of the concurrent benchmark (Garzón et al., 2026, EAAI 167:113593).
Three license-clean items are bundled directly:

- the gasoline and mayonnaise arrays extracted from the R `pls` package
  (GPL-2; redistribution with attribution is permitted — see
  `data/processed/NOTICE`);
- our own self-collected FT-NIR mixture data (`data/pentane_ccl4/`,
  CC BY 4.0);
- the simulated pretraining mixtures and their compositions
  (`data/pretrain_corpus/`, CC BY 4.0).

After retrieval, every loader in `src/datasets.py` works unchanged.

## Main benchmark (13 tasks)

| Dataset | Source | License / terms | Retrieval and placement |
|---|---|---|---|

## Self-supervised pretraining corpus inputs

`scripts/build_pretrain_corpus.py` (fixed seeds, deterministic) assembles the
9339-spectrum corpus from: the four main-benchmark datasets above (unlabeled
spectra only), the mayonnaise set (bundled, GPL-2), 98 NIST infrared reference
spectra, and 6000 simulated mixtures (compositions bundled in
`data/pretrain_corpus/`).

| Input | Source | License / terms | Retrieval |
|---|---|---|---|
| NIST IR reference spectra (98 compounds) | NIST Chemistry WebBook (SRD 69) | Free for use with citation; **do not redistribute** | `python scripts/fetch_nist_ir.py` → `data/NIST_IR/nist_ir.npz` (polite, cached, one request per compound) |
| Simulated mixture spectra (6000) | generated by `src/simulate.py` from the component library | CC BY 4.0 (ours) | **Bundled** in `data/pretrain_corpus/sim_compositions.npz`; regenerate with the corpus builder |

## Notes

- Eigenvector datasets (diesel, corn) require a free account on the vendor
  site; the download is a standard CSV/MAT export that needs no conversion.
- The R `pls` package can be obtained from CRAN
  (<https://cran.r-project.org/package=pls>); the bundled npz files were
  extracted from `gasoline.RData` and `mayonnaise.RData` without modification
  of the underlying arrays.
- Where a landing page states no explicit license (diesel, corn, EVOO), the
  datasets have a long history of research use in the chemometrics literature,
  but redistribution is not permitted and commercial use requires
  authorization from the provider.
